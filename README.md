ORBIT: DT Boards Action Office Process Automation and Management
Overview
The ORBITbranch is dedicated to documenting, analyzing, and automating the DT Boards Action Officeprocesses for the United States Space Force (USSF). This repository provides a comprehensive framework for managing the selection boards, improving project management awareness, and identifying opportunities for automation. The goal is to streamline workflows, reduce bottlenecks, and ensure compliance with documented guidance.
